# Cinemato — Advertising Inventory & Media Specifications

This specification sheet standardizes all physical and digital advertising formats supported on **Cinemato**, ensuring seamless execution between Brands, Cinema Concession Teams, and Projectionists.

---

## 1. Concessions & Packaging Media

### 1.1 Popcorn Bucket Custom Sleeves
* **Format Overview:** High-retention tactile branding wrapped around regular, medium, or large popcorn buckets. Handled for ~90-120 minutes per patron.
* **Standard Bucket Sizes:**
  - **Medium (85 oz / ~2.5L):** Top Dia: 185mm | Bottom Dia: 145mm | Height: 180mm
  - **Large (130 oz / ~3.8L):** Top Dia: 215mm | Bottom Dia: 165mm | Height: 210mm
  - **Jumbo / Tub (170 oz / ~5L):** Top Dia: 235mm | Bottom Dia: 175mm | Height: 230mm
* **Artwork Specifications:**
  - **Color Mode:** CMYK only (Pantone matching optional)
  - **Resolution:** 300 DPI minimum at 100% scale
  - **File Format:** Print-ready vector PDF or high-res TIFF
  - **Bleed:** 5mm on all sides, 10mm glue-flap allowance
  - **Food-Grade Safety:** Food-grade vegetable-based inks only (compliant with FDA / FSSAI standards)
* **Pricing & Minimums:**
  - Standard Batch Unit: Sold in increments of 1,000 / 3,000 / 5,000 / 10,000 units.
  - Typical Cinema Rate: $0.20 – $0.35 per tub (or INR 8 – 15 per tub).

### 1.2 Beverage Cups & Sleeves
* **Standard Cup Sizes:** 16 oz, 24 oz, 32 oz cold drink paper cups.
* **Artwork Specifications:** CMYK, 300 DPI, curved die-line template, moisture-resistant coating.
* **Pricing & Minimums:** Sold in batches of 2,000+ units.

## 2. On-Screen Projection Media

### 2.1 Interval Gold-Spot (30-Second Video)
* **Format Overview:** Played immediately when the feature film halts for interval, capturing peak audience attention before lights rise.
* **Technical Specifications:**
  - **Resolution Options:**
    - 2K DCI Flat: `1998 x 1080` (Aspect Ratio: 1.85:1)
    - 2K DCI Scope: `2048 x 858` (Aspect Ratio: 2.39:1)
    - 4K DCI Flat: `3996 x 2160` / Scope: `4096 x 1716`
  - **Frame Rate:** 24.000 fps (DCI Cinema Standard) or 25.000 fps
  - **Container / Codec:** Digital Cinema Package (DCP) unencrypted, or Apple ProRes 422 HQ / MP4 (H.264/H.265) for Cinemato auto-transcoding
  - **Audio Specifications:**
    - Channels: 5.1 Surround Sound (L, R, C, LFE, Ls, Rs) or Stereo 2.0
    - Audio Loudness: Standard cinema loudness `-24 LKFS / LUFS` (±1.0 LUFS)
    - Sample Rate: 48 kHz / 24-bit PCM
* **Censorship / Certification:**
  - Valid censor certificate (e.g. CBFC certificate in India, MPAA compliance in US) required as lead-in slide.
* **Pricing Metric:** Per screen / per week (or per show).

### 2.2 Pre-Show Reel (10s – 30s Slide or Video)
* **Format Overview:** Rotates in the pre-show reel during audience ingress (15–20 minutes before movie start).
* **Technical Specifications:** Available as silent slide (JPEG/PNG 1998x1080) or audio-video reel.

## 3. Lobby, Concourse & Physical Activations

### 3.1 Lobby Standees & Cutouts
* **Standard Dimensions:**
  - Standard Roll-up Standee: `3 x 6 feet` (36" x 72")
  - Large Die-Cut 3D Standee / Photo Booth: `6 x 6 feet` or `8 x 6 feet`
* **Material Requirements:** High-density star flex, sunboard (5mm with back easel support), or vinyl mounting.
* **Pricing Metric:** Per unit / per week.

### 3.2 Digital Poster Kiosks (DOOH in Lobby)
* **Standard Dimensions:** 55" to 75" Portrait LCD/LED screens.
* **Resolution:** `1080 x 1920` (9:16 Vertical Full HD), 30 fps MP4 or high-res JPG.
* **Pricing Metric:** 10-second loop slot / per week.

### 3.3 Experiential Sampling Stalls & Atrium Kiosks
* **Format Overview:** Physical activation booth (6x6 ft or 10x10 ft) in cinema atrium for live product sampling, test drives, or app downloads on weekends.
* **Pricing Metric:** Per day / per weekend (Friday – Sunday).

## 4. In-Auditorium Ambient Formats

### 4.1 Seat Backrest Stickers & Headrest Decals
* **Dimensions:** 150mm x 100mm (placed on back of each seat facing viewer behind).
* **Material:** Removable vinyl (residue-free adhesive that does not damage cinema upholstery).
* **Pricing Metric:** Per auditorium / per month.

### 4.2 3D Glasses Sleeve / Sticker
* **Dimensions:** 40mm x 15mm sticker on side arm or branded paper envelope containing glasses.
* **Pricing Metric:** Per 1,000 units.
