## 1. Brand Mission & Core Positioning
**Cinemato** is the modern ad-tech marketplace connecting **Cinema Hall Owners & Managers** with **Brand Advertisers & Business Owners**.
Traditional in-cinema marketing is slow, manual, and dominated by local brokers with opaque rate cards. Cinemato standardizes inventory into transparent, self-serve, escrow-protected booking packages with verified proof of performance.

---

## 2. Official Logo System: Hand-Drawn Sketch Interpretation (Black on White)
Based directly on the approved hand-drawn brand sketch:
* **The Logotype Structure (`C I N E M A T O`):**
  * **C**: Bold geometric cinema arc.
  * **I**: Architectural **filmstrip column** with vertical rails and horizontal sprocket perforations.
  * **N, E, M, A, T**: Clean, heavyweight cinema-marquee sans letterforms.
  * **O**: Vintage **movie projector film reel spool** with circular rim, central axle, and 5 radial drive cutouts.
  * **The Unspooling Ribbon**: An undulating physical film ribbon extending from the reel 'O', sweeping smoothly beneath the letters `T A M E N` to anchor into the 'I'.
* **Primary Color Scheme: Black on White (`#0A0E1A` on `#FFFFFF`):**
  * Created for ultra-high contrast, physical print packaging (popcorn bucket sleeves, cup wraps), box-office tickets, contracts, and digital headers.
  * **Vector Files:**
    - `branding/cinemato-logo-black-on-white.svg` (Primary brand logo lockup)
    - `branding/cinemato-reel-symbol.svg` (Standalone film-reel icon mark)

## 4. Typography Standards
* **Display / Brand Mark:** `Space Grotesk` (Weights: 700 Bold, 800 ExtraBold).
* **Headings & Sections:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` / `Outfit` (Weights: 600 SemiBold, 700 Bold).
* **Body & Financial Tables:** `Inter` / `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Weights: 400 Regular, 500 Medium, 600 SemiBold).

---

## 5. Usage & Co-Branding Rules
1. **Never distort or stretch** the film reel 'O' or the ribbon arc.
2. **Clear Space:** Maintain clear space around the wordmark equal to at least half the height of the letter 'C'.
3. **Black on White Rule:** For all official invoices, popcorn tub production print proofs, and exhibitor contracts, the Black on White lockup must be used.
