# Cinemato — Brand Identity & Design System Guide

## 1. Brand Vision & Positioning
**Cinemato** is the next-generation advertising marketplace bridging the gap between **Cinema Hall Owners / Managers** and **Brand Advertisers**.
We eliminate the friction, opacity, and middleman brokerage of traditional offline advertising by delivering programmatic inventory discovery, dynamic cinema rate-cards, escrow-protected payments, and verified proof-of-performance.

---

## 2. Core Brand Attributes & Archetype
- **Brand Archetype:** **The Magician + The Ruler**
  - *The Magician:* Evokes the wonder, larger-than-life scale, and unskippable sensory experience of cinema.
  - *The Ruler:* Establishes order, transparency, financial security, and standard rate enforcement in an otherwise chaotic market.
- **Brand Voice:**
  - **High-Impact & Cinematic:** Exciting, bold, screen-oriented.
  - **Empowering & Commercial:** Speaks directly to ROI, footfall conversions, and audience recall.
  - **Auditable & Secure:** Built on escrow trust, verified proof, and transparency.

---

## 3. Official Taglines
- **Primary Tagline:** `"Where Brands Meet the Big Screen."`
- **Secondary Tagline (B2B Advertisers):** `"Unskippable Offline Advertising, Simplified."`
- **Secondary Tagline (Cinema Partners):** `"Monetize Your Cinema Real Estate On Autopilot."`

## 5. Typography Standards
- **Display & Headings:** `Space Grotesk` (Weights: 600 SemiBold, 700 Bold, 800 ExtraBold)
  - Geometric, punchy, cinema marquee feel with modern tech sharpness.
- **Body & Interface:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` or `Inter` (Weights: 400 Regular, 500 Medium, 600 SemiBold, 700 Bold)
  - Ultra-legible at small sizes for complex rate cards, specs, and financial ledgers.
